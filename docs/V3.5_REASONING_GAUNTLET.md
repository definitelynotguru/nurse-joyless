# V3.5 Reasoning Gauntlet

V3.5 focused on high-volume reasoning audits and calibration, not new visual features.

## What changed

- Recalibrated identity scores to avoid multiple fake 100/100 archetypes.
- Added stricter Dragon Spam gating: fewer than three Dragon-type members no longer becomes Dragon Spam.
- Tuned Stall scoring so Dondozo/Garganacl-style anchors do not automatically make a team Stall.
- Retuned Balance/Bulky Offense reads for mixed teams with pivots, anchors, and breakers.
- Added dynamic Rain advice that references only actual team members, avoiding stale Bolt/Kyurem leakage.
- Kept suggestion cards actionable with copy-set and swap actions.
- Added the V3.5 Gauntlet test harness.

## Gauntlet results

- Teams tested: 320
- Average automated reasoning score: 100.0/100
- Teams at or above 90: 320/320

## Categories covered

- Hazard Stack Fat Balance
- Offensive Balance / mixed balance
- Jack-of-all-trades flawed balance
- Dragon Spam
- Rain Offense
- Sun Room / Trick Room
- Bad Fire stack
- Passive fat / stall-ish teams
- Six-sweeper offense
- No-win field-control spam

## Important caveat

The 300+ team gauntlet is an automated heuristic audit. It checks for consistency, impossible archetypes, score inflation, rain-dependency leakage, and category-specific reasoning failures. It is not a substitute for live ladder results or official Showdown simulation.
